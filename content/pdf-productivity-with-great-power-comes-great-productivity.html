Nutrition of Teenage Girls and Women in Humanitarian Crises

Adolescent girls and women face heightened nutritional risk during displacement, where disrupted food systems, limited healthcare access, and competing household demands compound micronutrient deficiency. This report synthesises field data across twelve humanitarian settings and models the intake gap against reference requirements for iron, folate, and vitamin A over the crisis window.
