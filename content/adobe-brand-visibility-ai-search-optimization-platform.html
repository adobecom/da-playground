Questions about getting found in AI search.

What is Adobe Brand Visibility? Adobe Brand Visibility is Adobe's unified generative engine optimization (GEO) product for enterprise brands. It enables teams to measure brand presence across AI-generated answers, identify optimization opportunities through SEO-powered analysis, deploy improvements at the CDN edge without engineering, collaborate across the full marketing organization, and prove business impact through native integration with existing analytics environments. How is this different from an SEO tool? Adobe Brand Visibility is built for the AI era. While traditional SEO tools optimize for search engine rankings, Brand Visibility focuses on how AI systems — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, and others — discover, cite, and represent your brand. It connects SEO signals to AI citation outcomes because AI-generated answers are built on the same foundations as search. You get both dimensions in one platform. What makes the CDN-edge deployment capability unique? Adobe Brand Visibility intercepts AI agent requests at the CDN layer and serves optimized content in minutes — with no CMS update, no engineering sprint, and instant rollback. This is categorically different from a content publishing workflow. Optimizations target AI agents only and have no effect on human visitor experience or existing SEO signals. How does Brand Visibility connect AI visibility to revenue outcomes? Adobe Brand Visibility integrates natively with Adobe Analytics and Customer Journey Analytics. CDN log verification provides server-level confirmation that AI bots read your updated content, and that signal is connected to revenue, conversions, and engagement inside the reporting environment your organization already uses. No custom tagging, no separate reporting silo. What AI platforms does Brand Visibility track? Brand Visibility tracks brand visibility across ten LLM families, including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews, and Microsoft Copilot, with competitive share-of-voice benchmarking against up to five direct competitors. Coverage spans more than 25 languages with state and city-level breakdowns. Is Adobe Brand Visibility only for Adobe Experience Manager customers? No. CDN-edge deployment is compatible with Fastly, Akamai, and Cloudflare — it's not exclusive to Adobe Experience Manager. The intelligence, opportunity identification, and attribution capabilities work across technology stacks.
